A colossal storm rumbles above Aerópolis, the archipelago of sky‑borne islands suspended over billowing clouds. Darkened clouds swirl around the jagged silhouettes of floating citadels, their spires etched in shimmering gold, while flashes of violet lightning crackle across the horizon, illuminating the city’s verdant rooftop gardens and the gleaming turquoise bridges that link the islands. The storm’s electric breath throws shards of red and blue light across the wet, glassy surfaces of the floating towers, creating a dramatic chiaroscuro that makes the entire scene feel alive with tension. The composition centers on a central island, its horizon framed by a swirling storm, rendered in a hyperrealistic yet painterly style that emphasizes dramatic contrast and depth.
